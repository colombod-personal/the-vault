The Vault guide

The Vault keeps your Magic: The Gathering collection and decks in one place, and lets the assistant you already use answer questions from your real cards, with the sources shown.

What it does for you

  • See what your collection is worth. Today's value, how it moved, what you paid.
  • Know which decks you can build. Each deck against what you own: what is missing and what it costs to finish.
  • Ask your assistant. It can read your collection and decks, check the rules against the real text, and tell you what is legal. Every answer says where each fact came from.
  • Keep it yours. Private by default. You can download everything or delete everything at any time.

The Vault is free and unofficial. It is not made by, or endorsed by, Wizards of the Coast.

In this guide

This guide is for people who use apps, not command lines. It covers the ChatGPT desktop app and Claude first.

How this guide is written

A step appears here only after someone ran it on the real app and saw it work. A page that is still marked being written has no steps yet, on purpose: a wrong instruction is worse than none. While the pages are being written, the Vault's own Connect page has the current instructions.

Need help, or want something added?

Use the Vault's support page to report a problem or ask for a feature. Please do not put your collection or any private details in a report.
